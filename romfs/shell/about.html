About Brewser

Home

Build once. Run on PC, mobile, and Switch.

Brewser is an online platform for creators, tinkerers, and developers — a curiosity-driven playground for sharing your ideas and projects with the world.

If you've ever built something clever and then watched it vanish into the void, you're exactly who we made this for.

Nintendo Switch homebrew HTML CSS JavaScript WebGL WASM
One codebase, three screens Build modern web applications with contemporary Web APIs that are cross-compatible with the Brewser runtime for Nintendo Switch. Write once, and your work runs across PC, mobile, and modded Switch consoles, no native toolchain.
Bringing hidden work into the light Too often a great project's final destination is a GitHub repository, buried among thousands. Brewser connects tinkerers with the people who actually want to see their work, your project deserves better than page 12 of a search result.
A home for the demoscene Brewser doubles as a demoscene environment. Publish a polished WebGL experience, a shader experiment, a procedural visual, or a little interactive demo, and share it with a community that appreciates the craft.
From the browser to the console Whatever you're building — a hardware experiment, a graphical demo, or a full-blown web app — Brewser gives your work a place to live, an audience to reach, and a runtime that follows it from the browser to the console.

Built for the physical and the interactive

Brewser is designed around how you create, connect, and interact with the world beyond the screen. Using established browser technologies, you can talk directly to smart devices and sensors from your PC, phone, or Switch — all from a standard web application, with no native build and no platform lock-in.

  • Sensors in real time Read an accelerometer or gyroscope as it moves.
  • Drive hardware Talk to a microcontroller or your own custom build.
  • Live audio Capture, process, and play sound on the fly.

Keep the brew going

Brewser runs on passion. Donations are voluntary gifts toward development costs, coffee and keeping the journey going!

Privacy note

Brewser may use network access to load websites, fetch app catalog information, or download app files when the user chooses to use those features.

Brewser itself does not intentionally collect personal information through this About page. Apps, websites, or third-party services opened through Brewser may have their own privacy practices, terms, cookies, analytics, or network behavior.

Users should review permission prompts carefully, especially for apps that request network access, storage access, WASM, or other special capabilities.
