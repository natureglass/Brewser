line-height

Baseline paragraph at the default block multiplier (1.4×). Two lines, two lines, two lines so you can compare the leading against the tighter / looser blocks below.

Tight (1.0): lines should be nearly touching. The multiplier inherits — anything inside (em, strong) keeps the same spacing because they don't re-set line-height.

Loose (2.0): very airy leading, twice the font-size between baselines.

Fixed 30px: leading is always 30px even though the paragraph's font-size is 18, so spacing is independent of font-size.

Percentage 150% → multiplier 1.5. Should land midway between the default and the loose block above.
