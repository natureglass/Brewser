border-radius

Validates rounded rectangles for backgrounds + borders and clip-to- rounded for <img> / <canvas> through the live-DOM render path. Any visible divergence from real-browser border-radius output (corrupted image bands, missing clip, inline-block layout mismatch) is a live-overlay bug-of-record.
