Full WebGL 2 Conformance Runner

Runs the full Khronos WebGL 2 conformance corpus inline within this page. Press A to start; press + (Plus) to abort mid-run.

Many Khronos tests assume browser APIs (Image, video, document.createElement, etc.) we don't implement. Failures here are expected and reported as data, not bugs. Per-test logs are written under sdmc:/switch/brewser/logs/full-webgl2-conformance/; the aggregated summary lands at sdmc:/switch/brewser/logs/full-webgl2-conformance.log.
