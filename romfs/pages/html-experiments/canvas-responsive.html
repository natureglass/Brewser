Responsive canvas

This canvas's script reads canvas.width and canvas.height and draws something that fills the entire surface at any size. Use the buttons below to toggle either fullscreen mode (exit with L + R on the controller).

What to look for

  • Inline (this page): canvas at 640x320 with the rings filling its space.
  • Tap Toggle full-screen canvas: chrome hides, canvas fills the screen at 1280x720, the readout changes to match. Rings stay centred and proportional.
  • Tap Toggle full-screen: chrome hides, the page itself fills the screen. Scroll still works (right stick / D-pad).
  • Press L + R at any time to return to normal.
